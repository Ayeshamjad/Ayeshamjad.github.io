Building AI That Actually Ships

I build AI systems that are meant to work beyond the demo. A lot of AI projects look impressive until you give them messy documents, multilingual data, or real users — that's the space I enjoy working in.

I'm Ayesha Amjad, an AI Engineer and Researcher at NUST with 2 years of hands-on experience building production LLM systems, generative models, and RAG pipelines. I've delivered agentic workflows and fine-tuned models for multiple clients, with published research and top competition placements in multilingual AI.

What sets me apart is the ability to bridge research and real-world deployment. I don't just prototype, I ship. From LoRA/PEFT fine-tuning and multi-agentic orchestration, to production APIs and full-stack applications, I own the full pipeline from model to product.

My research addresses a real infrastructure gap: Urdu, spoken by 230M+ people across South Asia, has no large-scale handwritten dataset, making OCR for millions of documents impossible. I'm developing generative pipelines that eliminate the need for large handwritten corpora, making it feasible to build OCR systems for any low-resource South Asian script from scratch, where none previously existed. I'm especially interested in problems where AI has to deal with real-world, unstructured information rather than clean datasets and perfect inputs.
